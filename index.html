Frequently asked questions

How do I scan my gear?

Open the Scan tab and tap Take photo to use the camera, or Upload photo to choose one from your library. Then tap Scan image. Analysis usually takes under a minute.

How do I get the best results?
  • Use good lighting and avoid heavy shadows or glare on cymbals.
  • Make logos and printed text (like cymbal stamps or drumhead logos) visible and in focus.
  • Overhead or angled shots of the whole kit work well. For a specific piece, get closer.
An item was identified incorrectly. Why?

Drummit uses AI image analysis, which can make mistakes, especially when logos are hidden, blurry or worn. Each result shows a confidence level and the reason for it. Treat results as a starting point and double-check before buying or selling gear.

Where are my saved scans and gear stored?

Only on your device. Drummit has no accounts and doesn't keep your scans on a server. If you delete the app, your saved scans and gear list are deleted too (unless they're restored from a device backup).

How do I delete a scan or item?

Saved scans and items live in the app on your device. Remove them in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Does Drummit need an internet connection?

Yes, to scan. Photos are analyzed by a server. You can browse saved scans and your gear list offline.

Does Drummit cost anything?

No. Drummit is free, with no ads and no account required.

What are the store links?

"Research and buy" links open retailer websites such as Sweetwater, Guitar Center and Reverb in your browser. Those sites are run by third parties and have their own terms and privacy policies.
