Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Drummit iOS app ("Drummit", "the app") handles your information. Drummit is developed by Max Brayman ("I", "me").

The short version Drummit has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Photos you scan are sent for analysis and are not stored on my server. Saved scans stay on your device.

Photos you scan

When you scan a photo, the app sends the image over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Drummit's server. The server forwards it to Google's Gemini API, which analyzes the image and returns the identified equipment. The server does not save your photos or the results. It passes them through and discards them once the request is finished.

Google processes the image to provide the analysis under its Gemini API terms. Google may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse, as described in those terms. Drummit does not allow Google to use your photos to train or improve its products. See Gemini API Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Please don't scan photos that contain information you don't want shared, such as people's faces, documents or addresses. Drummit only needs the gear in the photo.

Data stored on your device

If you choose to save a scan, the photo and its results are stored locally on your device. Items you star are also stored locally. This data isn't sent to me. It's included in your device's backups the same way as other app data, and it's deleted when you delete the app.

App integrity checks

Drummit uses Firebase App Check, a Google service, together with Apple's App Attest to verify that requests come from the genuine Drummit app on a real device. This helps prevent abuse of the scanning service. It uses device attestation information provided by Apple and doesn't identify you personally. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

Server logs

Like most online services, the server and its hosting provider (Fly.io) may automatically record basic technical information about requests, such as IP address, time and error details. This information is kept only briefly, for security and troubleshooting, and is not used to identify or track you.

What Drummit does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names or email addresses are collected in the app.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • Drummit doesn't access your location.

Permissions

Drummit asks for camera access only so you can take photos to scan. It uses the photo picker to let you choose images from your library, which gives the app access only to the photos you pick.

Links to other websites

Results include links to third-party retailers. When you open one, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Drummit is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete saved scans in the app, or delete the app to remove all data stored on your device. Because photos aren't stored on Drummit's server, there's nothing held there to delete. If you have questions or requests about your data, contact me.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
